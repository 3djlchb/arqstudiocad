== ARRAY in the main window, on main ab94cc8

The real MainWindow, 1366 x 740 on the X11 desktop (a 1098 x 347
physical-pixel viewport with the default panels, scaling 1). The renderer
the application chooses (OpenGL ES on the GT 730, "gpu") or the CPU through
MainWindow's safe mode, as --safe-mode ("cpu"). A generated drawing of N
lines (line i from (i mod s, i div s), s = ceil(sqrt N), half a unit long)
is opened, all of it selected, and ARRAY typed with its answers: 10 rows,
10 columns, spacing s+10 both ways, angle 0. Times from the command typed:
the copies made and reported ("command"); the events handled and the first
frame painted ("frame"). Then zoom to extents and 57 pan or zoom frames
(each a redraw and a repaint), and undo to its frame. Three runs each,
medians; pan and zoom are median / 95th percentile of each run.
