Machine as in operations.txt, with one change: since a reboot that day
the machine has a new monitor, HDMI-1, 1920 x 1080 at 60 Hz (480 x 270 mm);
the reference page had it at 1366 x 768. Measured on main at c19705b (after
#214), built by `uv sync`; fresh XDG folders and LANG=C.UTF-8 for every
run; nothing heavy running (checked before and after each pass).

Why: after the reboot, `gpu_viewport_check.py` showed the GPU viewport's
95th percentile near 32 ms, against 16.8-25.1 ms in earlier sessions, on
main at fc5d76b and with #214 alike (TESTING_AND_PERFORMANCE.md, "Overlay
painting without a QPointF a point"). With the maintainer's agreement the
screen was switched with xrandr between its 1920 x 1080 at 60.00 Hz and
1368 x 768 at 59.88 Hz (the mode nearest 1366 x 768 this monitor offers),
four passes alternating, then put back to 1920 x 1080. Each pass ran
`gpu_viewport_check.py` (a 1200 x 650 viewport, 100,000 entities, 57
frames after 3 warm-ups) and the startup harness below.

== Startup and opening, at both sizes

Harness (SHA-256):
  startup_child.py   54481148880e3bab1a3e26e5625e9ad49ce8041ed3b8641f53904e796a94415f
  startup_timing.py  0ae59d218e281c8cba480a7b9d67a4f2c5adfc58eadd2e5be6950385ce54d3b7

Written again from the method of 2026-11-11 and 2026-11-15 (the earlier
scripts were not kept): a copy of application.run, each start a new
process; marks from the launch (the parent's clock just before it starts
the child) at the window shown (a), the renderer's Choice used (b), the
card survey done (c), and the drawing, asked for at (a) by OPEN's
background path, opened: DocumentOpened (d). Five starts, medians. Then
the application shown at 1200 x 800, idle 1.5 s after its renderer is
chosen, opens the drawing five times by OPEN's path, closing it in
between: from the ask to DocumentOpened, to OPEN's CommandFinished and to
the first frame (events handled, the viewport repainted); medians. The
drawing is generated: 100,000 LINEs, line i from (i mod 500, i div 500) to
(+0.5, +0.5), as reference_machine_report.sh times. The renderer chosen
was OpenGL every time.
