== (1A) The polar diagnostic, repeated

TESTING_AND_PERFORMANCE.md, "Polar ARRAY diagnostic comparison": 100
lines i from (10+i%10, i//10) to (+0.5, 0), center (0,0), 100 items over
+360 degrees including the source; base (10,0) when not rotating. The
grouped workflow is COPY at zero offset and ROTATE of the new ids, or
COPY at the base's orbital offset; the atomic one is polar_array(ids,
(0,0), 100, tau, rotate, base). Each sample is checked: 10,000 entities,
the last item's copy of line 0 from (10cos a, 10sin a), a = 0.99 tau, to
(10.5cos a, 10.5sin a) rotating or (+0.5, 0) not, the ARRAYPOLAR history
entry, and undo to 100 entities. Garbage collection off; 2 warm-ups and
10 samples a run; five runs, the workflow going first alternating.

== (2) ARRAYRECT's orientation preview

Method as 2026-11-23/stretch-partial-arcs.txt: the same window and
renderers; 100,000 generated lines on a 3-unit grid, viewed so the whole
array shows (x 0..390, y 150..537); synthetic QMouseEvent moves on a
loop, each sample a move plus a repaint, 60 samples after 3 warm-ups.
"Idle" with no command; "preview" at "Specify array orientation" after
10 rows, 10 columns, 40 and 40, the 10 x 10 array turning about (0,0) as
the cursor moves.
  many  the 10 x 10 block of lines at x 0..27, y 150..177: 100 sources;
        the preview's work budget shows 20 of the 99 cells, with its
        notice (2,109 overlay strokes).
  one   the line at (0,150) alone: all 99 cells (109 strokes).
Then: cancelled, checked against the same selection shown with no
command (the selection stays after a cancel, as AutoCAD's); then run
again with 0 typed as the angle, checked and undone.

Cursor frames, ms, median (p95) of each round:
           Renderer  Idle                 Preview
many       CPU       1.57-1.65 (2.16-2.73)  26.70 (28.68) 26.99 (27.51) 26.96 (27.54)
           GPU       16.40-16.54            12.81 (13.14) 12.95 (13.11) 12.82 (13.02)
one        CPU       1.73-2.15 (2.44-3.05)  2.74 (3.17)   2.90 (4.20)   2.72 (4.32)
           GPU       16.42-16.55            16.29 (16.45) 16.37 (16.44) 16.29 (16.42)

On the GPU every frame is paced by the display; a median below one
refresh shows where the frame lands in the vsync, not less work.

Every run: the preview shown while the prompt was up; after the cancel
the history, the entity count and the overlay were those of the
selection alone; typing 0 made 9,900 (many) or 99 (one) copies in one
ARRAYRECT step, and undo restored the drawing.

== Verdict, against the agreed criteria

  (1A) 0.434 rotating, 0.736 keeping orientation: accepted.
  (1B) 9,900 copies on screen in 123-130 ms (limit 250), 99,000 in
       657-707 ms (limit 1,500); GPU frames at the display's rate, CPU
       frames at most 18.6 ms p95 (limit 33); counts, place and undo
       right: accepted.
  (2)  The preview at most 28.7 ms p95 a CPU frame (limit 33) and at the
       display's rate on the GPU; commit, cancel and undo right:
       accepted.

The margin of (2) on the CPU is small: 20 cells of 100 lines cost about
25 ms a frame more than the cursor alone, the 2,000 preview paths drawn
by QPainter. A larger budget would pass the limit on the floor; the
budget, not the machine, keeps it under.
Not covered: a hand on a real mouse, and ProgeCAD (Windows).
